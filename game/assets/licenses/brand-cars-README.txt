Detailed "brand" cars (BMW M5 CS, BMW M8, Dodge Challenger R/T, Tesla Roadster)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Source: https://github.com/Vivekkk-1/3D-Models (repository published under the Boost Software
License 1.0, copy in brand-cars-BSL-1.0.txt). Car names and designs belong to their
respective manufacturers. Included for private, non-commercial use only, as requested by the
project owner.

The files in assets/vehicles/brand/ were converted with tools/brand_cars.gd: meshes merged per
material, decimated to ~30-50k triangles, textures reduced to 1024 px, wheels split into
wheel_<a/b>_<l/r> nodes.
