Realistic vehicles, boats and aircraft
======================================
Private, non-commercial fan project. Trademarks and designs belong to their owners.

Cars, service vehicles and boats (assets/vehicles/pack, assets/vehicles/boats_real, and the BMW
M3 GTR, Camaro, GMC Canyon, Dodge pickup, Porsche 911 GT3 R and McLaren F1 in assets/vehicles/brand)
come from the Motorpool project (https://github.com/Atul-Senapati/Motorpool), which prepared
these Sketchfab exports:
  - "Generic Passenger Car Pack" and "Generic Civil Service Vehicles Pack" (Sketchfab)
  - "McLaren F1 1993 By Alex.Ka." by Alex.Ka. (https://sketchfab.com/Alex.Ka.), CC BY-NC 4.0
  - garage cars and boat set: Sketchfab exports as listed by that project
Wheels were re-fitted from the project's catalogue, meshes decimated and textures resized
with Blender (bpy) for this game.

BMW M5 CS, BMW M8, Dodge Challenger R/T, Tesla Roadster, Ferrari 599: see
brand-cars-README.txt (github.com/Vivekkk-1/3D-Models, Boost Software License 1.0).

Aircraft (assets/aircraft):
  - A320, E190, Citation II (C550), PC-12, SR22, Bell 407, EC135: FlightAirMap-3dmodels
    (https://github.com/Ysurac/FlightAirMap-3dmodels), derived from FlightGear aircraft
    (FGMEMBERS, GPL) and fr24-3d-models.
  - Cessna 172: "Plane" by osmosikum, CC BY 4.0 (via github.com/vinny-palumbo/FlightSim).
  - Dassault Rafale M: via github.com/vinny-palumbo/FlightSim (see that project's model licence).
