Realistic characters (assets/people)
====================================
Mixamo characters (Adobe) redistributed in public GitHub projects. Converted with Blender
(FBX -> GLB, textures to JPEG/PNG) and retargeted on import to Godot's humanoid profile.

  Ch12.glb (Jason), Ch16.glb, Ch28.glb  - github.com/eseosapku/Interactive_Character_Experience
  Ch17.glb                               - github.com/CayneByron/CayneByron.github.io
  Ch07.glb (Lucia)                       - github.com/ezgiaslan99/Runner_Game_EAI
  Soldier.glb (SWAT, "Vanguard")         - github.com/mrdoob/three.js (examples/models/gltf)

Mixamo characters are (c) Adobe; see https://www.adobe.com/legal/terms.html (Mixamo) for their
terms. Private, non-commercial fan project.

Animations: Quaternius "Universal Animation Library" 1 & 2 (CC0), glTF export by J-Ponzo
(github.com/J-Ponzo/gltf-universal-animation-library).
